01 Overview & Application Identity

This Privacy Policy explains how 4-in-1 Photo Puzzle ("we", "us", or "our", Application Identifier: com.photopuzzle.game) handles information when you play our puzzle game across supported platforms (iOS, Android, and Web).

Our core commitment is privacy by design: 4-in-1 Photo Puzzle does not require user accounts, passwords, or personal registrations. We do not sell personal data, and we do not maintain centralized databases of player identities.

02 Zero Phone Storage & Personal Photo Access

4-in-1 Photo Puzzle does NOT access, request, scan, read, collect, or store any personal media from your device.

No Personal Phone Images Guarantee

The application does not request device camera permissions and does not read photos, albums, or media stored in your device's photo gallery. Your personal family photos, screenshots, and private files remain completely untouched and inaccessible.

All puzzle artwork featured in the game is either pre-packaged in the application build or delivered over the internet from royalty-free public art repositories, as detailed below.

03 Internet Image Delivery (Pixabay API)

To provide an exciting, infinite variety of puzzle stages, the game provides artwork through two transparent channels:

  • Stages 1 to 62 (Local Curated Masterpieces): Packaged directly within the game application files. These stages are completely playable offline without requiring any internet connection.
  • Stages 63 Onward (Infinite Online Stages): High-resolution, royalty-free wallpapers, photographs, and digital artwork fetched over encrypted HTTPS connections using the public Pixabay API.

When fetching web puzzles for Stage 63 and above:

  • The app transmits only generic keyword search parameters (e.g. "fantasy landscape", "neon digital artwork") to retrieve public, royalty-free images.
  • No personal user identity, device serial numbers, or location coordinates are sent to the image provider.
  • Image data is processed in device memory to generate jigsaw, sliding, shift, and rotation tiles for your active game session.

04 Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To ensure a seamless gameplay experience across sessions, 4-in-1 Photo Puzzle saves certain non-identifying game preferences and progress directly to your device's local storage:

Data Category Purpose Storage Location
Stage Progression Tracks which stages you have cleared and unlocked across Classic, Rotation, Sliding, and Shift modes. On-device local storage
Coin Balance Records earned reward coins from stage completions, used for hints and multipliers. On-device local storage
Difficulty Selection Preserves your chosen difficulty tier (Rookie, Pro, Elite, Master) until you manually change it. On-device local storage
Audio Preferences Remembers your toggle choices for Sound Effects and Gameplay Background Music. On-device local storage
How to Reset Your Local Data:

You have complete control. You can permanently erase all completed stages, coins, and configurations at any time by opening the in-game Settings modal and tapping "Reset All Progress", or by clearing application storage in your operating system settings.

05 Third-Party Advertising & SDKs

To keep 4-in-1 Photo Puzzle free for all players, the application may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK).

How Advertising Works

Ad networks may collect and process standard diagnostic device telemetry to deliver, measure, and optimize advertisements, such as:

  • Mobile Advertising Identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID or Apple IDFA).
  • Coarse IP address (used for approximate regional language/country delivery and fraud prevention).
  • Technical device specifications (device model, operating system version, screen resolution).
  • Ad engagement metrics (views, clicks, and rewarded video completion).

Managing Your Ad Preferences

You can opt out of personalized interest-based advertising or reset your advertising identifier directly on your mobile device:

  • On Android: Open Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.
  • On iOS: Open Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and disable tracking, or visit Apple Advertising to toggle off Personalized Ads.

06 Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

4-in-1 Photo Puzzle is designed as a family-friendly casual puzzle game suitable for audiences of all ages. Protecting the privacy of young players is of paramount importance to us.

  • We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).
  • The game does not require account registration, social login, or multiplayer chat functionality.
  • Where ad networks are configured for child audiences, advertisements are served in compliance with Google Play Families Policy and COPPA standards (contextual-only, non-personalized ads).

07 Data Security & Server-Free Design

Because 4-in-1 Photo Puzzle does not host user profiles, passwords, or personal image uploads on private cloud servers, the risk of data breaches concerning your personal identity is eliminated.

All local gameplay records are sandboxed securely within your device's operating system environment in accordance with standard Android, iOS, and modern web application security protocols.

08 Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or California), you may exercise legal privacy rights:

  • Right to Know & Access: You can verify what data exists. We only store non-identifying local game progress on your device.
  • Right to Erasure: You can wipe all local game data at any time via Settings > "Reset All Progress".
  • Right to Opt Out of Sale: We do not sell your personal data. You may limit ad personalization via your device OS settings.
