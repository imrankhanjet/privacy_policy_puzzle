01 Overview & Application Identity

This Privacy Policy explains how Photo Puzzle ("we", "us", or "our", Application Identifier: com.photopuzzle.game) handles information when you use our mobile and web applications across supported platforms (iOS, Android, and Web).

Our core philosophy is simple: privacy by design. Photo Puzzle is built as an offline-capable, client-side photo jigsaw game. We do not require an account, we do not maintain a central database of player identities, and we do not sell your personal data.

02 Camera & Photo Library Access

Photo Puzzle includes an interactive feature called "Create from Photo" that lets you turn any image from your device gallery or camera into a playable jigsaw puzzle.

Strict Client-Side Guarantee

All image slicing, rotation, resizing, and rendering algorithms execute 100% locally within your device's browser/runtime memory using standard HTML5 Canvas and Web APIs. Your selected images are NEVER transmitted over the internet, NEVER uploaded to external servers, and NEVER shared with any third parties.

Here is what happens when you use this feature:

  • Camera Permission: If you choose "Take Photo", the application requests permission to access your device camera strictly to capture a photo for puzzle generation. The photo remains on your device.
  • Photo Library / File Access: If you select an existing photo from your gallery, the application reads the file locally into temporary memory to slice it into puzzle pieces.
  • No Cloud Sync: We do not operate cloud servers for user photos. When you close the custom puzzle session or clear your session, the image data is freed from active memory.

03 Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To ensure a continuous gameplay experience, Photo Puzzle saves certain non-identifying game preferences and game states directly to your device's local storage (such as HTML5 localStorage or local app sandbox):

Data Category Purpose Storage Location
Game Progress Tracks which puzzles you have completed and unlocked across each difficulty level (Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert). On-device local storage
Star Points Balance Records your accumulated reward points earned from solving puzzles and used for hints. On-device local storage
Audio & Haptic Settings Saves your preferences for Sound Effects (on/off), Ambient Harmony music (on/off), and Haptic Feedback vibration (on/off). On-device local storage
Active Session State Saves in-progress puzzle piece positions so you can resume playing without losing your work. On-device local storage
How to Delete Your Local Data:

You have full control. You can permanently erase all progress, points, and saved states at any time by opening the in-game Settings modal and tapping "Reset All Progress", or by clearing your browser/app storage data in your operating system settings.

04 Third-Party Advertising & SDKs

To keep Photo Puzzle free to play, the application may include third-party advertising services, such as Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK).

How Advertising Works

When advertising is enabled, ad partners may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers and diagnostic technical data to deliver, measure, and optimize advertisements. This may include:

  • Mobile Advertising Identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID on Android or IDFA on iOS).
  • Coarse IP address (used for approximate regional geolocation and fraud prevention).
  • Device information (hardware model, operating system version, screen resolution, locale/language).
  • Ad performance metrics (impressions, clicks, and video completion rates).

Third-Party Privacy Policies

Google AdMob's data practices are governed by Google's Privacy Policy. For more information on how Google uses data from partner apps, please visit:

Google Privacy & Terms: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services ↗

Managing Your Ad Preferences & Opting Out

You can limit personalized advertising or reset your advertising identifier directly on your mobile device:

  • On Android: Go to Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID or enable Delete advertising ID.
  • On iOS: Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and disable tracking permissions, or visit Apple Advertising to turn off Personalized Ads.

05 Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Photo Puzzle is designed as a family-friendly casual puzzle game suitable for all ages. Protecting the privacy of children is of paramount importance to us.

  • We do not knowingly collect, request, or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).
  • We do not require account registration, social logins, or chat functionality.
  • If ad networks are configured for child-directed audiences, advertisements are served in compliance with Google Play Families Policy and COPPA guidelines (non-personalized, contextual-only ads).

If a parent or guardian believes that any personal information has inadvertently been provided, please contact us immediately so we can promptly address the situation.

06 Data Security & Server-Free Design

Because Photo Puzzle does not host user accounts, user photo databases, or personal profile servers, the risk of remote server breaches or data leaks concerning your photos is fundamentally eliminated.

All local game data is sandboxed within your device's operating system security boundaries according to standard Android, iOS, and browser security protocols.

07 Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your region of residence (such as the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or California), you may have legal rights regarding your data:

  • Right to Know / Access: You have the right to know what data is collected. As documented here, we only store local game progress and settings on your device.
  • Right to Deletion: You can erase all gameplay data anytime via the in-app "Reset All Progress" button or device application storage settings.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale / Sharing: We do not sell personal data. You can opt out of personalized interest-based advertising via your device settings as outlined in Section 04.
  • Non-Discrimination: Exercising your privacy rights will not result in any penalty or denial of service in Photo Puzzle.
